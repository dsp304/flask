How to Setup in Your Email Account

📬 Gmail / Google
  1. Click the filter icon in the Gmail search bar.
  2. Paste the keywords into "Has the words".
  3. Click "Create filter".
  4. Select "Forward it to" and enter your forwarding address above.
📧 Outlook / Microsoft
  1. Open Settings → Mail → Rules.
  2. Click Add new rule.
  3. Condition: Subject or body includes any of these words (paste keywords).
  4. Action: Forward to your forwarding address above.
